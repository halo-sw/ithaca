## Design Context

### Users
People comparing candidate homes, starting with Korean housing decisions and extending to examples around Meta HQ. Household members can have different work, school and everyday routines. Hackathon judges must be able to understand the same working product in a short English demo.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, approachable. The user's supplied Ithaca arch wordmark is the primary identity. Keep its hierarchy clear; Meta attribution belongs where it explains an actual model contribution.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain Ithaca's warm, restrained interface and illustrated 3D neighbourhoods. The user explicitly requires regional consistency with the established Gangnam view and rejects mismatched night/fallback renderings, blurry maps, camera jitter and indiscriminate logo labels. Day, night, loaded imagery and missing imagery should feel like states of the same product. Preserve real geographic differences.

### Design Principles
1. Keep ground, road, roof and facade material rules consistent across regions and loading states.
2. Preserve geographic detail at night; lighting changes must not erase the neighbourhood.
3. Label schematic fallback truthfully while retaining the same palette, camera and controls.
4. Show actual events and calculated values; proposed routines and missing data remain visible.
5. Use steady motion and readable evidence, with branding only where it contributes meaning.
